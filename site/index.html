01 · The mechanism

The GPU fingerprint is one integer.

PyTorch’s CUDA random-number kernels size their launch grid from two device properties — and that grid is the only way the hardware enters the random stream. Reproduce the grid, and you reproduce the “fingerprint”, on anything.

grid = min( ceil(N/256) , SMs × (threads_per_SM / 256) )

Click a device — the wall, the barcode and the number all change.

1,184= 148 × 8
B200 / GB200 · the entire information content of “GB200”
148 SMs× 8 blocks/SM = 1,184 cells
Every “hardware fingerprint” his method can read is one of these cells.

The first 101,376 values of the Ada stream and the GB200 stream are identical. They diverge only where the launch-grid cap begins to bite.

04 · The statistics

1,385σ and 0.999995 cannot both be true.

Significance grows with sample count: z = r·√N / √(1−r²). A correlation of 0.999995 over a billion weights is ~107σ. To get 1,385σ at that correlation you need about 19 independent samples. The two headline numbers are not measurements of the same thing.

10,000,000σ his 0.999995, applied to 10⁹ weights

His reported range, 49–1,385σ, sits below the 369,435σ our RTX-4070-initialized model produces after a single optimizer step. The number measures sample size and distance from init — not hardware.

05 · The inference

Init is not training. It costs about a hundred dollars.

Initialization is a one-time, I/O-bound step every training run performs anyway. Moving it to rented Blackwell hardware is a cloud invoice, not a smuggling operation — and it leaves exactly the fingerprint he describes.

“do not establish the physical location of the chips, how they were acquired, or whether export control regulations were violated” — the report’s own executive summary

A layout match is consistent with a legal rented node, a partner lab, a cloud region, a deliberate decoy, a stale config, or a genuinely smuggled cluster. It cannot tell them apart. That is not a forensic result; it is a Rorschach test with a p-value.

06 · The code

He published the method. We ran it.

It reproduces his entire table on this machine — every seed, every SM count, every median r. The Philox kernel is real, the rows are pinned Hugging Face weights, the licence is MIT.

Then his own README narrows the claim to the initialization:
